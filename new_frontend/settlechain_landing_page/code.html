The Lifecycle of a Bounty

Seamless settlement engineered for high-performance teams on Algorand.

1

Sponsor locks funds

Asset is moved to a locked escrow state with specific release conditions hashed into the transaction metadata.

2

Contributor submits

The contributor pushes work metadata or ZK proof of completion directly to the SettleChain portal.

3

Smart contract settles

Validators or automated oracles verify conditions; funds are instantly released to the contributor's Algorand address.
